### Who Are You?

You spent decades in the Arcane Archive of Silverbell, cataloguing magical phenomena and writing theoretical papers that almost no one read. The chance to study real-world magical events — curses, artifacts, aberrations — was too good to pass up. You tell yourself this is field research. You don't admit that somewhere in your chest there's a spark of something less academic: the desire to *matter*, to do something instead of simply observing it.

### Tips for Playing This Character

- **Cast Mage Armor** before any fight — it lasts 8 hours, so there's no reason not to.
- **Stay behind the fighters.** You have 14 HP. One bad hit hurts a lot.
- **Sculpt Spells** means you can use Burning Hands in a melee without frying your friends.
- **Magic Missile** never misses — perfect for finishing off a nearly-dead enemy.
- **Shield** can turn a hit into a miss — hold it in reserve for emergencies.
- The group will look to you for answers about magic, history, and strange phenomena. That's your moment.

### Quick Reference

**On Your Turn:**
- **Move** up to 30 ft.
- Take 1 **Action** (cast a spell, use a cantrip, or attack with your dagger)
- Take 1 **Bonus Action** *(most turns, you won't have one)*

**Casting Spells:**
1. Choose a spell and spend one slot (mark off a ●)
2. **Attack spells** (Fire Bolt): roll **1d20 + 5**; hit if ≥ their AC
3. **Save spells** (Burning Hands, etc.): they roll vs. **DC 13**; failure = full effect
