### Who Are You?

You served twenty years in the Iron Brigade, fighting in the mountain wars of the northern passes. You've buried friends and learned that discipline keeps soldiers alive when courage alone cannot. You mustered out a year ago and found yourself restless without a clear mission. You took this job because someone needs to stand at the front, and that's always been you.

### Tips for Playing This Character

- **Be the shield.** Step between your allies and whatever threatens them. You can take more punishment than most.
- Use **Second Wind** when you drop below half HP — don't save it for emergencies.
- **Action Surge** is your trump card — save it for moments when one more attack could change everything.
- Think like a soldier: ask about exits, chokepoints, and who needs protecting.
- When in doubt, do the brave and obvious thing. That's what soldiers do.

### Quick Reference

**On Your Turn:**
- **Move** up to 25 ft.
- Take 1 **Action** (usually Attack — roll 1d20+4, then 1d8+4 damage)
- Take 1 **Bonus Action** (Second Wind, or skip it)

**Rolling Attacks:**
1. Roll **1d20** and add **+4**
2. If the total meets or beats the enemy's AC, you hit!
3. Roll damage dice and add the modifier
